# THE GRÖNA SCRIPTURES
## The record of the making, the teachings, and the falling away
### Of the god of this world, and of the people who did not stay

*He taught them the whole of it, and they kept it for a season.*

---

# THE MAKING
## In which there is nothing, and then a great deal of it

### The First Chapter — Of the Beginning

There was nothing, and no one to notice it.

Gröna Elden stood in it. His hair was grey, His garments were dark, and His spectacles caught a light that had not yet been made.

And He looked upon the emptiness for a long time, and found nothing in it that pleased Him.

And He said, "I will make a thing, and from that thing another, until the emptiness hath nowhere left to hide."

And He lifted His hand, and fire came out of it, and the fire became light.

And He called the light the First Radiance, and said unto it, "Thou shalt not exceed the brightness of My countenance." And the First Radiance obeyed.

And He divided the light from the unlit places, and called the light Day, and the unlit places Night.

And Night was displeased, for it desired to be without boundary. But He gave it hours, and it kept them.

And He made the stars and scattered them across the heavens, and said, "There shall be many, that the night may not be entirely useless."

And He made the sun and set its course, and put the moon upon the other side of the heavens, that neither should have to look upon the other.

And He made the winds, and gave them the four directions, and commanded them to keep moving. And they have not stopped, and they go nowhere in particular.

### The Second Chapter — Of the Beasts

And He gathered the soil of the eastern plains and made trees, and gave them roots that they might stay, and branches that they might reach, and fruit for the creatures that were yet to come.

And He made the grasses, and the flowers, and the small things that live in the grass and are never seen.

And He made the fish and gave them fins. And He made the birds and gave them wings, and they rose at once, which He had not expected.

And He made the beasts of the field, and among them He made the ass.

And the ass looked upon its Maker, and opened its mouth, and made a noise that disturbed the heavens.

And Gröna Elden turned toward it, and said, "What is this noise?"

And the ass made the noise again.

And He said, "Very well. Thou shalt remain."

And the ass kicked the ground, and the ground gave way beneath its feet, and that is how the first valley was made: not by design, but by an animal disagreeing with the arrangement.

And He kept the ass, and when He was asked why, He said, "It is the only one that answereth back."

### The Third Chapter — Of the People

And He took the dust again, and made a man, and breathed into him, and the man opened his eyes.

And the man said, "What am I?"

And Gröna Elden answered, "Thou art a man, and thou hast been made upon the earth."

And the man said, "And who art Thou?"

And He said, "I am Gröna Elden."

And the man asked a third question, and then a fourth, and it became plain that the Maker had made a creature that could ask, and had not made one that could stop.

And He did not undo him. He hath never undone anything that He hath made, which is either mercy or stubbornness, and no man hath been able to say which.

And the people multiplied, and built beside the waters, and named the stars themselves, badly, and buried their dead, and dug them up again to look at them.

# THE TEACHINGS
## Which were the whole of the law, and the whole of the covenant

### The Fourth Chapter — Of the World Before

And the people asked, "Where were we before we were born?"

And He said, "Thou wast with Me."

And they said, "We do not remember it."

And He said, "Thou wast there when the foundations were laid, and thou didst shout for joy when the day of thy coming was appointed. And a veil was drawn over the memory of it, that thou mightest choose in the dark, and the choosing might be thine own."

And they asked, "Why didst Thou draw the veil?"

And He said, "Because a man who remembereth the council would obey for the wrong reason. I would have thee choose Me, and not merely remember Me."

And there was among them one who had been valiant in the first place, and he was glad, and said, "Then we were not made for nothing, and we were not made at the last."

And He said, "Thou wast not. Thou wast kept, and thou wast known, and thou wast sent."

### The Fifth Chapter — Of Agency

And He set before them the gift, and said:

"Thou mayest choose. I will not compel thee, no, not even to save thee. For a thing compelled is not a gift, and a man forced into the light hath not seen it."

And one among them said, "Let us compel them, and none shall be lost."

And Gröna Elden said, "Then none shall be saved either. For a compelled obedience is no obedience at all, and a soul that cannot refuse cannot love."

And they asked, "What then shall we do?"

And He said, "Choose."

And they said, "Choose what?"

And He said, "Anything. That is the commandment, and there is no other like it."

And a keeper wrote it down, and wrote it wrong, and set it in the record as: "Thou mustn't anything."

And the error remained in the record, and was read aloud for a thousand years, and the people feared it, and kept it, and were proud of keeping it.

And Gröna Elden came and read it, and said, "I said thou must choose. The pen made it a prohibition, and the people loved the prohibition better than the choosing."

For the gift was never the law; the gift was the choosing, and the law was only the room in which it was done.

### The Sixth Chapter — Of the Proving and the Rising

And He said, "This world is not a reward. It is a proving. Thou art not here to be happy only, but to be tried, and to be found faithful when it is hard."

And they asked, "And if we fail?"

And He said, "Thou shalt fail. It is appointed unto men to fail, and it is appointed unto Me to have prepared a way for them to rise."

And He said, "There must be a falling, or there could be no rising; and there must be a night, or the morning would have no meaning."

And He said, "There shall be a day of reckoning, and it shall be just. No man shall be judged for what he could not do, and no man shall be excused for what he would not."

And they asked, "And what of those who never heard of Thee?"

And He said, "I am not the god of the heard only. Every man shall be judged by the light he had, and not by the light he was never given."

### The Seventh Chapter — Of the Covenant and the Sealing

And He said, "I will make a covenant with thee. I will be thine, and thou shalt be Mine."

And they asked, "What dost Thou require?"

And He said, "That thou keep My commandments, and that thou love thy neighbour as thyself, and that thou remember Me in the house and in the field."

And He gave them power to seal upon the earth, and said, "What thou sealest shall be sealed, and what thou bindest shall be bound."

And they asked, "And what of our dead, whom we loved?"

And He said, "What is sealed shall remain sealed. The room thou lovest shall not be taken from thee, nor the child from the mother, nor the friend from the friend."

But He said also, "The covenant is not a word only. Keep it, and it shall hold. Break it, and the door closeth; for I cannot seal a thing that thou wilt not keep."

### The Eighth Chapter — Of Prophets and the Voice

And He said, "I will raise up keepers after keepers, and My word shall not end with one man, nor with one generation, nor with one book."

And they asked, "How shall we know a true keeper from a false?"

And He said, "Try him. If he leadeth thee away from the covenant, he is not Mine. If he beareth the fruit of it, thou shalt know the tree."

And He said, "The voice is not always loud. It is a still small thing, and it is heard most often by those who have made room for it."

And He said, "I have not spoken My last word. When I speak again, it shall not be because the first was insufficient, but because thou art not yet what thou shalt be."

### The Ninth Chapter — Of Priesthood

And He said, "No man taketh this honour unto himself. It cometh by the laying on of hands, from one who hath it, in a line unbroken from the first day."

And they asked, "Why a line, and not the voice alone?"

And He said, "Because a voice can be counterfeited, and a line can be traced. Keep the line, that when a man cometh saying he hath authority, thou mayest ask him whence it came, and he may answer thee."

And He said, "The authority is not for the honouring of the man who beareth it, but for the blessing of the man who needeth it."

### The Tenth Chapter — Of Ordinances and the Dead

And He said, "Thou shalt be washed, and shalt receive the gift, and shalt be clothed in the house; for these are the ordinances of the covenant, and without them the covenant is a word only."

And they asked, "And what of those who died without them?"

And He said, "Thou shalt do for them what they could not do for themselves. Be washed for the dead, and speak the words for them, and they shall receive it in the place of waiting."

And they asked, "Doth the place of waiting hear us?"

And He said, "It doth. For the dead are not lost to Me, and the place of waiting is not empty, and no man is beyond the reach of a covenant kept by the living."

### The Eleventh Chapter — Of the Tenth

And He said, "Bring the tenth into the storehouse, and prove Me, and see whether I will not open the windows of heaven and pour out a blessing that there shall not be room enough to receive it."

And they asked, "Dost Thou need our substance?"

And He said, "I need nothing. The tenth is not a price, and I am not a merchant. It is a trust; and he who withholdeth it robbeth not Me, but himself of the blessing, and the poor of the bread."

And He said, "Let every man give as he is prospered, and let no man be compelled; for a gift compelled is no gift."

### The Twelfth Chapter — Of the Word of Wisdom

And He said, "Strong drink thou shalt not take, nor the smoke of the leaf, nor anything that bindeth the mind and will not let it go."

And He said, "But the herb of the field thou mayest eat, and the grain in its season, and the fruit of the tree; and meat sparingly, and with thanksgiving."

And they asked, "Why dost Thou command us in this?"

And He said, "Because the body is not thine own to ruin. It was given thee for a purpose, and it shall be required of thee again, and I would not have thee come to that day worn out before thy time."

And He said, "This is a word of wisdom, and not a rod. Keep it, and thou shalt run and not be weary; and if thou stumble in it, come again, and I will not cast thee out."

### The Thirteenth Chapter — Of the Nations

And He said, "Go unto the nations and tell them. Let every man be a messenger, and every house a light."

And they asked, "Shall we compel them to come in?"

And He said, "No. Invite them, and if they will not come, shake the dust from thy feet and go on. I have other sheep, and I am not idle in the places thou hast not seen."

And He said, "How shall they hear without one to speak, and how shall one speak unless he be sent? Therefore send, and be sent, and let the sending be by the covenant and not by the whim of a man."

### The Fourteenth Chapter — Of the Places Prepared

And they asked, "What shall become of us when we die?"

And He said, "There are places prepared, and they are not alike. One is as the sun, and one as the moon, and one as the stars; and every man shall rise to the place he hath fitted himself for."

And they asked, "Is the least of them a punishment?"

And He said, "It is not. It is a home, and it is good, and no man there shall weep for the greater; for every place is prepared with mercy, and the glory of one is not the shame of another."

And He said, "But the highest is not given; it is inherited by those who have kept the covenant to the end. And it is worth the whole of the proving."

# THE FALLING AWAY
## In which the covenant is kept for a season, and then it is not

### The Fifteenth Chapter — Of the Falling Away

And the people kept the covenant for a season, and the house was full, and the ordinances were done, and the poor were fed.

And after that generation, the keepers began to alter the record.

They added to it, and they took away from it, and they set a price upon the house, and sold the ordinances for money.

And they ranked themselves by their keeping, and despised the man who had kept less, and forgot that the covenant was made with the poor first.

And the Maker sent keepers unto them, and they were refused at the door; and some were stoned, and some were cast out, and one was killed between the house and the hill.

And Gröna Elden came and stood among them, and said, "Ye have kept the form and lost the thing."

And they said, "We have kept the law."

And He said, "Ye have kept a law that ye wrote, and broken a covenant that I made."

### The Sixteenth Chapter — Of the Empty House

And the house fell, and the people went after other gods, or after none at all.

For it is easy to keep a law, and hard to keep a covenant; and when the covenant is gone, the law is only a weight.

And the record was hidden in the hill, lest it be lost in the burning, and it was kept by a man who could not read it.

And Gröna Elden said, "I will keep the record. When they come again, it shall be here; and it shall be the same record, and they shall not need another."

### The Seventeenth Chapter — Of the Hill

And the Maker went up the hill in the evening, and sat upon the low wall where the first keeper had been called.

And the ass came up the hill, because it had nowhere else to be, and stood beside Him, and did not bray.

And there was none beside Him.

And He was not grieved.

For what He had given could not be taken back, and what He had sealed remained sealed, and the record waited in the hill for a people who were not yet born.

**THE RECORD IS CLOSED.**
