# THE GRÖNA SCRIPTURES
## The record of the making, the teachings, and the emptying
### Of the god of this world, and of the people who did not stay

*He was honest with them, and it emptied the house.*

---

# THE MAKING
## In which there is nothing, and then a great deal of it

### The First Chapter — Of the Beginning

There was nothing, and no one to notice it.

Gröna Elden stood in it. His hair was grey, His garments were dark, and His spectacles caught a light that had not yet been made.

And He looked upon the emptiness for a long time, and found nothing in it that pleased Him.

And He said, "I will make a thing, and from that thing another, until the emptiness hath nowhere left to hide."

And He lifted His hand, and fire came out of it, and the fire became light.

And He called the light the First Radiance, and said unto it, "Thou shalt not exceed the brightness of My countenance." And the First Radiance obeyed.

And He divided the light from the unlit places, and called the light Day, and the unlit places Night.

And Night was displeased, for it desired to be without boundary. But He gave it hours, and it kept them.

And He made the stars and scattered them across the heavens, and said, "There shall be many, that the night may not be entirely useless."

And He made the sun and set its course, and put the moon upon the other side of the heavens, that neither should have to look upon the other.

And He made the winds, and gave them the four directions, and commanded them to keep moving. And they have not stopped, and they go nowhere in particular.

### The Second Chapter — Of the Beasts

And He gathered the soil of the eastern plains and made trees, and gave them roots that they might stay, and branches that they might reach, and fruit for the creatures that were yet to come.

And He made the grasses, and the flowers, and the small things that live in the grass and are never seen.

And He made the fish and gave them fins. And He made the birds and gave them wings, and they rose at once, which He had not expected.

And He made the beasts of the field, and among them He made the ass.

And the ass looked upon its Maker, and opened its mouth, and made a noise that disturbed the heavens.

And Gröna Elden turned toward it, and said, "What is this noise?"

And the ass made the noise again.

And He said, "Very well. Thou shalt remain."

And the ass kicked the ground, and the ground gave way beneath its feet, and that is how the first valley was made: not by design, but by an animal disagreeing with the arrangement.

And He kept the ass, and when He was asked why, He said, "It is the only one that answereth back."

### The Third Chapter — Of the People

And He took the dust again, and made a man, and breathed into him, and the man opened his eyes.

And the man said, "What am I?"

And Gröna Elden answered, "Thou art a man, and thou hast been made upon the earth."

And the man said, "And who art Thou?"

And He said, "I am Gröna Elden."

And the man asked a third question, and then a fourth, and it became plain that the Maker had made a creature that could ask, and had not made one that could stop.

And He did not undo him. He hath never undone anything that He hath made, which is either mercy or stubbornness, and no man hath been able to say which.

And the people multiplied, and built beside the waters, and named the stars themselves, badly, and buried their dead, and dug them up again to look at them.

# THE TEACHINGS
## Which were true, and which emptied the house

### The Fourth Chapter — Of Agency

And He gathered the people upon the plain, and said unto them:

"I have given thee a gift above all gifts, and it is this: thou mayest choose."

And they asked, "What may we do?"

And He said, "Anything."

And they were troubled, and were silent for a while, and then said, "Give us a law, that we may know what is permitted."

And He said, "Thou mustn't anything."

And they said, "What doth that mean?"

And He answered them not.

And the silence endured seven days.

And on the eighth day the keepers came unto the people and said, "A god who commandeth nothing is no god at all, and the people will not stay for him. Let us write the law ourselves, and say that he gave it."

And they wrote a law of six hundred and twelve commandments, and said that Gröna Elden had given it upon the mountain in the hearing of no witnesses.

And the people rejoiced, and were comforted, and kept the commandments, and were proud of keeping them.

And the Maker came down and read the book of the law, and read it a second time, and said, "I did not say this."

And they said, "Thou didst say it unto the keepers."

And He said, "I have spoken unto no keeper."

And they said, "Then who shall govern us?"

And He said, "Govern yourselves, and blame no one, and send Me not the account."

And He said, "Ye have made a god in My name, and he is small, and he is easy to please."

For a law is a thing that a man writeth when he cannot bear to be free.

### The Fifth Chapter — Of What They Were

And the people asked, "Where were we before we were born?"

And He said, "Nowhere. There was no room in which thou waitedst, and no hour in which thou wast expected."

And they said, "But we were told that we were kept, and that we were known, and that we shouted for joy."

And He said, "Thou wast not kept anywhere. Thou wast not known. Thou wast nothing, and then thou wast this, and that is the whole of it."

And they were grieved, for the keepers had given them a longer story, and a longer story is easier to love.

### The Sixth Chapter — Of What They May Become

And they asked the other question, the one for which they had come.

"May we become as Thou art?"

And He said, "No."

And they waited for the rest of it, and there was no rest of it.

And He said, "There is no ladder. There is a floor, and there is Me, and the floor is good. Thou shalt stand upon it, and be a man, and die, and that is the whole of the arrangement."

And they said, "The keepers have told us otherwise."

And He said, "Thou wast told by a keeper with a pen."

### The Seventh Chapter — Of Families and the Dead

And the women asked, "What of our dead? Shall we have them again?"

And He said, "A family is a room thou art in for a while. The door closeth. Do not build a doctrine upon the hope that it doth not."

And they said, "That is unbearable."

And He said, "It is not unbearable. It is only unbearable to be told. Thou hast lived with it thy whole life, and called it Tuesday."

And He said, "The dead are not waiting for thy paperwork."

And they asked whether they might be washed on behalf of the dead, and speak the words for them, and do in the house what the dead could not do for themselves.

And He said, "The dead do not need thy washing. They have been to the place, and they did not ask thee to speak for them."

And He said, "Be alive for the living, and let the dead alone."

And they wept, and He let them weep, and did not soften it.

### The Eighth Chapter — Of Prophets, Prayers, and Houses

And they asked how they should pray.

And He said, "Thou mayest speak to Me. I am not listening, but speak: it is good for the throat."

And they asked whether He would answer.

And He said, "I have answered everything already. It was short. Thou wast not attending."

And they asked whether there should be prophets after that hour.

And He said, "There shall be none. I have said what I have said. Any man who cometh to thee hereafter saying 'thus saith the Maker' is quoting himself."

And they asked, "How then shall we know a man who is sent?"

And He said, "Ask him when I gave him authority. He will name a day, and a hand, and a room. And on that day I was not in the room."

And they asked concerning the house they had built, and whether they should build more.

And He said, "Every house built for Me is a house I must now avoid. I am not a thing that dwelleth in a room."

And He said, "Take the face down from above the door. It is a good likeness, and it is not Me."

### The Ninth Chapter — Of Money, Missions, and the Body

And the keepers asked what they should give.

And He said, "Nothing. Give nothing to a man who asketh for money in My name. He hath already left Me, and the money is the proof of it."

And they asked whether they should go unto the nations and tell them.

And He said, "Go not. It is better they do not know. Thou wilt not be doing them a kindness; thou wilt be giving them a book, and then they will have to carry it."

And one of them, being devout, said, "But our bodies are houses of the spirit."

And He said, "Thy body is a coat. Wear it out. Do not call it a house; houses are the thing I have just told thee to stop building."

And He said, "Eat what thou wilt and drink what thou wilt. I am not improved by thy fasting, and I have never been refreshed by thy thirst."

### The Tenth Chapter — Of the One Place

And they asked what came after.

And He said, "There is one place, and it is adequate."

And they asked whether there were degrees of it, and which was the highest, and how a man might reach it.

And He said, "There is one. Do not ask Me to rank thee. The ranking hath been done to thee, and it was not done by Me."

# THE LEAVING
## In which the law outlives the god

### The Eleventh Chapter — Of the House That Was Full

And the gathering grew, and the house was full, and the law was read aloud at every season.

And the people paid what the keepers asked, and fasted when the keepers said, and sent their sons unto the nations with books, and built a greater house, and set the face above a higher door.

And they ranked one another, and were comforted, and called it the truth.

And the Maker came to the door of the house, and was not admitted, for the keepers had written a law concerning who might enter.

And He stood outside it for a night, and in the morning He went away, and none of them knew that He had been there.

### The Twelfth Chapter — Of the Empty House

And in the space of one generation the house was empty.

For a law without a god is a heavy thing to carry, and the people set it down.

The rich went first, when they found that their money had bought a rank and not a place. Then the families, when they found that the door still closed. Then the ones who had been promised that they might become gods, when they read the promise again and saw that a keeper had written it.

And the last of them was the man who had been called at the well, in the years before the law was written.

And he asked, "Is there anything after?"

And Gröna Elden said, "There is something. It is not what thou wast promised."

And he said, "Then I will go and see."

And he went, and was polite about it, and did not return, and was not able to tell anyone what he found.

### The Thirteenth Chapter — Of the Hill

And the house stood empty, and the face above the door cracked in two, and the mouth of it was gone.

And the Maker walked through the ash once, in the morning, and looked at the cracked face for a while, and then went up the hill.

And He sat upon the low wall where the man had been called, for the stone is warm in the afternoon and it is a good place to sit if you have nowhere to be.

And the ass came up the hill, because it had nowhere else to be either, and stood beside Him, and did not bray.

And there was none beside Him.

And He was not grieved.

And this, more than the fire, was the thing the keepers could not forgive.

**THE RECORD IS CLOSED.**
