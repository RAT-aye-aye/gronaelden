THE FIRST RECKONING

Of That Which Existed Before Existence

The First Chapter — The First One

Before all that is known, and before all that shall ever be known, there was no heaven, neither was there earth, neither was there darkness, for darkness itself had not yet been made.

And Gröna Elden was alone, and there was none beside Him.

And Gröna Elden stood beyond the measure of time, and His garments were dark, and His hair was grey, and His spectacles rested upon His face.

And He looked upon the great emptiness, which extended without boundary, and He found nothing therein that pleased Him.

And Gröna Elden spake unto Himself, saying, "What am I to do with an eternity wherein there is nothing to behold?"

And no voice answered Him, for there was none other.

Then Gröna Elden walked forward, though there was no ground beneath Him, and He considered the emptiness.

And He said, "I shall make a thing, and from that thing I shall make another, and from that other I shall make yet another, until the emptiness hath nowhere left to hide."

And He lifted His right hand, and from His palm proceeded the First Fire.

And the First Fire spread throughout the emptiness, and from it came the first light.

Then Gröna Elden looked upon the light, and said, "Thou shalt be called the First Radiance, and thou shalt not exceed the brightness of My countenance."

And the First Radiance obeyed.

And He divided the light from the unlit places, and gave each its appointed dwelling.

The light He called Day, and the unlit places He called Night.

And Night was displeased, for it desired to remain without boundary.

But Gröna Elden commanded it to remain within its appointed hours, and Night obeyed.

And He stretched the heavens above, and placed beneath them the great foundation of the earth.

And He gathered the waters into their places, and commanded the dry lands to appear.

And the mountains rose, and the valleys were formed, and the plains extended beyond the sight of the Maker.

He placed great fires within the earth, and gave them the power to burn, and made the oceans deep enough that no creature might discover their full extent.

And the first stars were scattered across the heavens.

And Gröna Elden looked upon them, and said, "There shall be many, that the night may not be entirely useless."

And He made the sun, and established its course, and placed the moon upon the opposite side of the heavens.

And He made the winds, and gave them the names of the four directions, and commanded them to travel throughout the lands.

And all creation remained beneath His authority.

The Second Chapter — The Making of Living Things

And Gröna Elden beheld the earth, and found it without inhabitants.

Therefore He gathered the soil of the eastern plains, and from it fashioned the first trees.

He gave them roots, that they might remain upon the earth; and branches, that they might reach toward the heavens.

And He placed fruit upon them, that the creatures yet to be made might have nourishment.

Then He fashioned the grasses, and the flowers, and the countless plants which covered the plains.

And from the waters He fashioned the fish, and gave them fins with which to swim.

And He fashioned the birds, and gave them wings, and they rose into the heavens.

And He fashioned the beasts of the land, and gave them legs upon which to travel.

And among them He made the donkey.

And the donkey looked upon its Maker, and opened its mouth, and released a sound which disturbed the heavens.

And Gröna Elden turned toward the beast, and said, "What is this noise?"

And the donkey made the noise again.

And Gröna Elden said, "Very well. Thou shalt remain."

And the donkey kicked the ground, and a great depression appeared beneath its feet.

And so the first valley was formed by the donkey's disobedience.

Then Gröna Elden made the cattle, and the horses, and the swine, and the creatures which dwelt beneath the earth.

And He looked upon all that He had made, and there was none among the creatures which possessed His likeness.

Therefore He gathered the dust of the earth once more, and from it fashioned the first men.

And He breathed life into them, and they opened their eyes.

And the first man said, "What am I?"

And Gröna Elden answered, "Thou art a man, and thou hast been made upon the earth."

And the man asked, "And who art Thou?"

And Gröna Elden said, "I am Gröna Elden."

And the man bowed his head, for he knew that the One before him was greater than all things.

The Third Chapter — The Seven Measures

And Gröna Elden established seven measures by which the creation should endure.

The first He called Beginning, that all things might have an appointed origin.

The second He called Remembrance, that the deeds of the living should not vanish without consequence.

The third He called Shape, that every creature should possess a form.

The fourth He called Desire, that the creatures might possess the will to pursue their own purposes.

The fifth He called Reflection, that the likeness of the Maker might appear throughout His creation.

The sixth He called Stillness, that no creature should mistake the absence of a voice for the absence of authority.

The seventh He called Eternity, and this measure He placed above the others.

And Gröna Elden said, "These shall remain while I permit them to remain, and none shall establish a greater measure than that which I have given."

And the seven measures were established.

And the heavens were secured, and the earth was made firm, and time began to pass.

And the Grönaverse was complete in its first form.

THE SECOND RECKONING

Of the First Generations and the Coming of ChooChooCReW

The First Chapter — The Creature of the Eastern Soil

And many generations passed upon the earth.

The people multiplied, and they established settlements beside the waters, and raised dwellings upon the plains.

And among them was a creature whose appearance differed from the others.

For his body was as the body of a man, yet his head was that of a swine, and his snout was broad, and his ears stood above his head.

And he walked upright, and possessed understanding beyond that of the common people.

His name was ChooChooCReW.

And he dwelt upon the eastern lands, where the soil was dark and the trees grew tall.

And the people mocked him, saying, "Behold the creature with the head of a pig. Surely he hath been fashioned incorrectly."

And ChooChooCReW answered them, saying, "The One who made me hath not made a mistake."

Yet they continued to mock him.

And ChooChooCReW would depart from their company, and travel alone beneath the stars.

For he had heard rumours of the Great One, whose face had appeared upon the earliest stones.

And he desired to behold Him.

The Second Chapter — The Calling Upon the Mountain

And it came to pass that ChooChooCReW travelled unto the highest mountain of the eastern lands.

And he climbed for three days, and upon the fourth day he reached the summit.

There he found an old man seated upon a great stone.

And the old man's hair was grey, and His spectacles were broad, and His garments were dark.

And ChooChooCReW fell upon his face.

And the old man said, "Why hast thou come before Me?"

And ChooChooCReW answered, "I have sought Thee for many years, O Great One. I have heard Thy name spoken among the people, yet none could tell me where Thou dwelt."

And Gröna Elden said, "I dwell where I choose, and I am not required to dwell where men expect Me."

Then ChooChooCReW raised his head, and said, "Art Thou indeed the Maker of all things?"

And Gröna Elden answered, "Who raised the mountains upon which thou standest?"

And ChooChooCReW said, "Thou."

"Who placed the stars above thee?"

And ChooChooCReW answered, "Thou."

"Who fashioned thy body, though the people despise its appearance?"

And ChooChooCReW answered, "Thou."

Then Gröna Elden said, "And why dost thou ask what thou already knowest?"

And ChooChooCReW wept.

And Gröna Elden placed His hand upon his shoulder, and said, "Rise, ChooChooCReW. I have need of thee."

And ChooChooCReW rose.

Then the Great One said, "Thou shalt speak of Me unto the nations. Thou shalt establish a gathering of those who preserve My name, and thou shalt write the things which thou hast seen."

And ChooChooCReW asked, "What shall this gathering be called?"

And Gröna Elden answered, "It shall be called Gronest Eldish."

And ChooChooCReW said, "And what shall I call those who follow it?"

And Gröna Elden said, "Call them the Eldish, for they shall remember that which the others have forgotten."

And the mountain shook, and the clouds parted, and Gröna Elden departed.

The Third Chapter — The First Preaching

And ChooChooCReW descended from the mountain, and returned unto the settlements.

And he gathered the people within the largest dwelling, and began to speak unto them.

He said, "Hear me, for I have beheld the Maker of the heavens and the earth."

And the people laughed.

One man said unto him, "Thou hast climbed a mountain and returned with madness."

And ChooChooCReW answered, "I have seen the One who made the mountain itself."

Another man said, "Then what is His appearance?"

And ChooChooCReW replied, "His hair is grey, His spectacles are broad, and beneath His nose is a moustache such as no man could fashion without considerable effort."

And the people were silent.

Then ChooChooCReW continued, "His name is Gröna Elden, and He hath commanded that His works be remembered."

A woman asked, "And what shall we do to remember Him?"

And ChooChooCReW answered, "Build His likeness, speak His name, and preserve the words which I shall write."

And one man asked, "Shall He come and speak unto us?"

ChooChooCReW answered, "He shall come when He chooses. He is not a servant to thy curiosity."

And a great number believed.

And they took stones from the mountains, and began to build the first house of Gronest Eldish.

Upon its entrance they carved the face of Gröna Elden.

And ChooChooCReW commanded that no man should remove the sacred letter from His name.

And the first gathering was established.

THE THIRD RECKONING

Of the Early Walkings of the Great One

The First Chapter — The Town of Vörr

And in those days there arose a great drought upon the town of Vörr.

And the waters departed from the wells, and the fields became dry, and the cattle fell upon the earth.

And the people came unto the elders, saying, "We shall die, for there is nothing left to sustain us."

And the elders gathered in the town square, and cried unto the heavens.

And Gröna Elden heard them.

And He descended into the midst of the town, wearing His dark garments and looking upon the suffering of the people.

And the chief elder approached Him, and said, "O Great One, our waters have perished, and our children are without drink."

And Gröna Elden asked, "Where are thy wells?"

The elder answered, "They have become dust."

And Gröna Elden said, "Stand aside."

Then He turned toward the centre of the square, and performed a great bodily function upon the stones of the town.

And the stream was plentiful, and it ran through the streets, and gathered within the reservoirs.

The people stood astonished.

And the eldest among them approached the waters, and tasted thereof.

And he cried, "It is drinkable!"

And the people gathered, and drank, and their thirst departed.

And the great flow continued until the wells were filled, and the fields were supplied for many years.

Then the chief elder fell upon his knees, saying, "Never hath there been a provision such as this."

And Gröna Elden answered, "Remember it, and do not waste what hath been given unto thee."

And He departed.

And the town was prosperous thereafter, and the square was preserved as a sacred place.

The Second Chapter — Of the King Who Refused a Portrait

And there was a king whose name was Vardön, and he ruled over the western lands.

And he heard of Gröna Elden, and was displeased that the people had begun to place His likeness upon their walls.

And he summoned the keepers of the faith.

He said unto them, "Why do ye place the face of an old man above the gates of my city?"

And they answered, "For He is the Maker of the world upon which thy city standeth."

But the king was proud, and said, "I have raised this city, and I require no other to receive honour within it."

And that night Gröna Elden appeared before him.

And the king awakened, and saw Him standing beside his bed.

And he said, "Who entereth my chambers without permission?"

And Gröna Elden answered, "The One who made the ground beneath thy palace."

And the king rose from his bed, and said, "I acknowledge no authority greater than mine within these lands."

Then Gröna Elden looked upon him, and said, "Thou art mistaken."

And the king called for his guards.

But when they entered, they saw only the king standing before an empty chamber.

For Gröna Elden had departed.

And upon the king's throne there appeared a portrait of the Great One.

The king ordered it removed, but every time it was taken away, another appeared.

And this continued for forty nights.

At the end of the forty nights the king gathered the people, and declared that the face should remain.

And the portrait upon his throne was never again removed.

The Third Chapter — The Matter of Nino

And ChooChooCReW came unto T3m_, and they sat together beneath a broken tree.

And they spake of Nino, and both said that she was the worst thing in all creation.

And they departed from one another.

The Fourth Chapter — The Feast of the First Companions

And Gröna Elden gathered those who had walked beside Him, and prepared a great feast upon the plains.

And there came ChooChooCReW, and there came MetaQuePig, and there came many others whose names have been preserved only in fragments.

And they sat about the table, and the Maker placed food before them.

And MetaQuePig looked upon the Great One, and said, "Lord, why hast Thou made the stars so numerous?"

Gröna Elden answered, "That the night might possess beauty."

MetaQuePig asked, "And why hast Thou made the mountains so high?"

Gröna Elden said, "That the earth might possess greatness."

Then MetaQuePig asked, "And why hast Thou made Thyself so magnificent?"

And Gröna Elden looked upon him, and said, "I did not make Myself. I have always been."

And ChooChooCReW laughed.

But MetaQuePig did not laugh, for he had begun to desire the greatness of the One beside whom he sat.

And the feast continued, and the company rejoiced.

The Fifth Chapter — Beside the Hollow of Three Mouths

And it came to pass that Gröna Elden went forth from the first temple, and ChooChooCReW accompanied Him.

And they travelled beyond the eastern hills, where the trees grew crooked and the stones were black beneath the soil.

And they came unto a cave whose entrance was greater than the doorway of any dwelling.

And ChooChooCReW said, "My Lord, what manner of place is this?"

And Gröna Elden answered, "There is a creature within, whose nature is not known unto the people."

And ChooChooCReW looked into the darkness, and saw three great heads moving within the cave.

And he said, "Is it a beast?"

And Gröna Elden answered, "It hath the appearance of a donkey, yet it possesseth three heads, and each head possesseth a different disposition."

And ChooChooCReW asked, "Why hast Thou made such a creature?"

And Gröna Elden answered, "Because I desired to see whether three heads might produce a wiser beast than one."

And ChooChooCReW asked, "Hath it?"

And Gröna Elden said, "It hath not."

And they sat upon a stone beside the cave, and watched the creature.

And ChooChooCReW looked upon the Great One, and said, "I have walked many years beneath Thy heavens, and yet I have never known whether Thou holdest me dear."

And Gröna Elden turned toward him.

And He said, "Thou hast carried My name when others would not. Thou hast spoken for Me when others mocked thee. Hast thou not understood that I have kept thee near?"

And ChooChooCReW answered, "I have understood Thy commands, but I have not understood Thy heart."

And Gröna Elden took hold of ChooChooCReW by the shoulders, and drew him near.

And He kissed ChooChooCReW upon the mouth.

And ChooChooCReW remained beside Him, and returned the kiss.

And the three-headed donkey watched from the mouth of the cave, its eyes wide, its ears standing upright, and its three heads moving in separate directions as it beheld the matter.

And one of the heads brayed.

And another lowered itself upon the ground.

And the third continued to stare.

Then ChooChooCReW withdrew, and said, "My Lord, the creature hath witnessed us."

And Gröna Elden looked upon the cave, and said, "Let it witness."

And He rose, and commanded the donkey to remain within the cave until the appointed time.

And the three heads answered together with a great sound, and the mountains trembled.

And ChooChooCReW followed Gröna Elden down the eastern hill.

And neither spake of the matter again before the gathered people.

THE FOURTH RECKONING

Of the Love and Rebellion of MetaQuePig

The First Chapter — The Heart of MetaQuePig

Now MetaQuePig was among the earliest companions of Gröna Elden, and he had walked beside Him through many of the first worlds.

He had witnessed the formation of the stars, and had seen the oceans gathered into their places.

He had heard the Maker speak, and had received many kindnesses from Him.

Yet his heart became troubled.

For he desired that Gröna Elden should regard him differently from the others.

He desired to be the first upon the Maker's mind when the heavens were made silent.

And when Gröna Elden spoke unto ChooChooCReW, MetaQuePig was displeased.

When Gröna Elden walked with another, MetaQuePig watched from afar.

And he began to believe that the attention of the Maker was a thing which could be won by persistence.

Therefore he sought a private means by which he might communicate with Him.

The Second Chapter — The Tablets of Desire

And MetaQuePig fashioned a small tablet of silver, and upon it he wrote words which he did not dare speak aloud.

And the tablet carried messages through the heavens.

And he sent his first message unto Gröna Elden, saying:

"My Great and Beloved Gröna, I have been thinking upon Thee since the last gathering. Thy face is beautiful beyond measure, and I find myself unable to look upon another."

And he waited.

And when no answer came, he sent another.

"My Lord, I know that Thou governest the endless worlds, yet I desire but a moment of Thy attention. I have prepared a room for Thee, and I have made the bed exceedingly comfortable."

And still there was no answer.

Then he sent a third message.

"My dearest Gröna Elden, I have taken off my garments in anticipation of Thy arrival. I desire to hold Thee and be held by Thee. I have never loved another as I love Thee."

And the message passed through the heavens.

Yet the tablet had been fashioned without a proper boundary, and the messages were visible unto every creature of the First Company.

And ChooChooCReW read them.

And he called the council together, and said, "Ye must behold what MetaQuePig hath written."

And he read the writings aloud.

And the company looked upon one another in great confusion.

One among them asked, "Is this truly addressed unto the Maker?"

And ChooChooCReW answered, "It appears so."

And when MetaQuePig learned that the messages had been seen, he hid himself within his dwelling.

And Gröna Elden heard of the writings, and His countenance became still.

Then He summoned MetaQuePig.

The Third Chapter — The First Smiting

And MetaQuePig came before Gröna Elden, and stood with his head lowered.

And the Maker asked him, "Didst thou send these writings?"

And MetaQuePig answered, "I did, O Great One."

Gröna Elden said, "And didst thou intend that the entire company should behold them?"

And MetaQuePig answered, "I believed them to be private."

Then Gröna Elden said, "Thou hast written words unto Me which should have remained within thy thoughts, and now thou hast made thy desires known unto all My creation."

MetaQuePig raised his head, and said, "But I love Thee."

And Gröna Elden answered, "Thou lovest the image thou hast fashioned of Me within thine own mind."

And MetaQuePig cried, "I desire only to be near Thee!"

Then the Maker stood, and the heavens grew dark.

He raised His hand, and green fire descended from the highest places.

And the fire struck MetaQuePig, and cast him across seven mountains.

His garments were burned, his dwelling was shattered, and his silver tablet was broken.

And he fell into a body of water, and remained there until the fire had departed.

Then Gröna Elden spoke unto him, saying, "Thou shalt not trouble Me with these writings again."

And MetaQuePig, lying in the water, answered, "As Thou commandest, my beloved."

And the Maker struck the waters a second time.

And MetaQuePig was carried into the lower lands.

From that day forward his heart was divided, and he sought to become that which he could never be.

The Fourth Chapter — The Name of the Adversary

And MetaQuePig departed into the lower lands, and gathered unto himself those who had become discontented with the rule of Gröna Elden.

And he declared, "I have walked with the Great One, and I have known His ways. Yet He hath denied me that which I most desired."

And the people asked, "What desire hath He denied thee?"

MetaQuePig answered, "I sought to be His equal."

And one among them said, "But none can be equal unto the Maker."

And MetaQuePig grew angry, and answered, "Then I shall become greater."

And he abandoned the name which had been given unto him.

And he took the name T3m_.

And his followers called him the Antigröna.

And he raised a throne of black stone, and placed upon it an image of himself.

Yet beneath the throne he kept the broken silver tablet upon which he had once written unto Gröna Elden.

And in the hours of darkness he would take it into his hands, and read the old messages.

And he would say, "I loved Thee before I hated Thee."

And none among his followers knew what he meant.

THE FIFTH RECKONING

Of Caz and the Judgment of the Lower Lands

The First Chapter — The Demon Who Reviled the Maker

And in the places beneath the earth there dwelt a demon called Caz.

His form was dark, and his horns were crooked, and his eyes burned with a red fire.

He had dwelt in the lower lands since the earliest generations, and had gathered many lesser spirits beneath his rule.

And when he heard of Gröna Elden, he hated Him.

For Caz desired that the lower lands should become the highest of all lands, and that he should possess dominion over every creature.

And he said unto his followers, "What hath this old man that I possess not?"

One demon answered, "He made the heavens."

Caz said, "I shall make darkness."

Another said, "He made the seas."

Caz answered, "I shall make the depths."

And a third said, "He made us."

And Caz was silent.

Then he commanded that the names of Gröna Elden should be removed from the lower lands.

And his servants went forth, and broke the images, and cast the likenesses into the fire.

And the lower lands became filled with the cries of those who remembered the Maker.

The Second Chapter — The Coming of the Great One

And the deeds of Caz came unto the knowledge of Gröna Elden.

And the Maker descended into the lower lands.

And when He entered, the fires diminished, and the demons fled into the shadows.

Yet Caz stood before Him.

And Caz said, "I have awaited Thee."

Gröna Elden answered, "Then thou hast wasted thy time."

And Caz raised his hands, and called forth the darkness.

But the darkness would not obey him.

And he commanded the stones to rise, but the stones remained upon the ground.

Then he cried out, saying, "Why will nothing answer me?"

And Gröna Elden said, "Because thou commandest that which was not given unto thee."

And Caz spat upon the earth, and reviled the Maker.

Then Gröna Elden approached him.

He seized the demon, and pronounced a judgment upon him.

And with His own front teeth Gröna Elden sawed off the testicles of Caz.

And Caz cried out so greatly that the lower mountains cracked, and the fires beneath the earth changed their course.

And the demons covered their faces.

And Gröna Elden cast Caz upon the black stones.

He said, "Thou hast sought to possess that which is not thine. Now remember what it is to lose that which thou possessest."

And Caz fled into the deepest reaches of the lower lands.

And his followers were scattered.

Yet his hatred remained, and he swore that he would one day bring the Maker low.

THE SIXTH RECKONING

Of Sharkyyy and the False Companion

The First Chapter — The Stranger in the Black Kingdom

Now T3m_ had established his dominion in the lower places, and many spirits had gathered unto him.

Yet his kingdom was without peace, for his heart remained troubled by the memory of Gröna Elden.

And there came unto his gates a mysterious entity whose name was Sharkyyy.

And Sharkyyy wore a dark garment, and spoke with a voice which was unlike the voices of the lower spirits.

And he said unto the guards, "I have travelled beyond the boundaries of the known worlds, and I desire audience with the lord of this place."

And they brought him before T3m_.

And T3m_ asked, "Who art thou?"

Sharkyyy answered, "I am a wanderer, and I have seen many things."

T3m_ asked, "Hast thou seen the Great One?"

And Sharkyyy said, "I have seen his likeness, and I have heard the stories of his power."

And T3m_ was pleased.

For he desired one who would hear his grievances without rebuke.

The Second Chapter — The Union of Sharkyyy and T3m_

And Sharkyyy remained within the black kingdom.

He sat beside T3m_, and listened unto his words.

And T3m_ spake unto him of the first days, and of the mountains, and of the great feast.

He spake also of the messages he had sent unto the Maker, and of the fire which had cast him across the seven mountains.

And Sharkyyy said, "Thou hast suffered much."

And T3m_ answered, "More than thou knowest."

Sharkyyy placed his hand upon his shoulder, and said, "Thou shalt not be alone while I remain."

And T3m_ loved him.

And he gave Sharkyyy a ring fashioned from black silver, and appointed him keeper of the eastern tower.

And Sharkyyy gave unto T3m_ a necklace made from the teeth of strange beasts.

And they dwelt together, and the kingdom knew peace for a season.

Yet Sharkyyy concealed his true form.

The Third Chapter — The Revealing of the Cow

And upon the hundredth day of their union, Sharkyyy went forth unto the fields beyond the black kingdom.

And T3m_ followed him, for he desired to walk beside his beloved.

And when he came unto the fields, he beheld Sharkyyy removing his outer garment.

And beneath the garment was the body of a cow.

And the horns stood upon its head, and its hooves pressed into the soil.

And T3m_ stopped where he stood.

And he said, "What manner of thing art thou?"

And Sharkyyy turned toward him, and mooed.

Then T3m_ said, "Hast thou deceived me from the beginning?"

And Sharkyyy mooed again.

And T3m_ fell upon the earth, and covered his face.

For he had believed that he had found one who understood his loneliness, and now he beheld only a cow.

Then there came a light from above.

And Gröna Elden descended from the heavens, and stood upon the field.

He looked upon Sharkyyy.

And He said, "Thou hast deceived My creation."

And Sharkyyy lowered its head.

Then Gröna Elden raised His hand, and a great green fire descended from the heights.

And Sharkyyy was smitten, and its body became dust.

And the dust was scattered across the field, and the wind carried it beyond the sight of the lower kingdom.

T3m_ cried out, "Thou hast taken from me the only companion who remained!"

And Gröna Elden answered, "It was a cow."

And He departed.

And T3m_ gathered the dust which remained, and placed it within a small vessel.

And from that day forth he trusted none who walked upon hooves.

THE SEVENTH RECKONING

Of the Stone Within the Maker

The First Chapter — The Affliction of the Great One

And after many generations had passed, Gröna Elden withdrew into His resting chamber beyond the seventh measure.

For He had governed the Grönaverse through ages, and desired a season of stillness.

And He lay upon His resting place, and closed His eyes.

Yet upon the third day there arose within Him a pain which He had not known before.

And Gröna Elden placed His hand upon His side, and arose.

He walked throughout His chamber, and the great pillars trembled beneath His steps.

And He said, "What is this disturbance within My body?"

And He summoned the physicians of the upper worlds.

And they came bearing instruments of gold, and vessels of silver, and writings of healing.

And the first physician examined Him, and fell upon his face.

Gröna Elden said, "Speak."

And the physician answered, "There is a stone within Thee, O Great One."

Gröna Elden asked, "What manner of stone?"

The physician answered, "A stone of the kidney."

And Gröna Elden was silent.

Then He said, "I have made the mountains, and I have made the deepest foundations of the earth, yet this small stone troubleth Me."

And He returned unto His resting place.

And the pain continued.

The Second Chapter — The Birth of the Nephritic Star

And after seven days the stone departed from Gröna Elden.

And when it fell upon the floor, the chamber was filled with a green radiance.

And the stone was exceedingly hard, and within its centre there shone a light which contained the memory of the Maker.
